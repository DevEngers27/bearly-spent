# MoneyQuest for parents

## Create family, sign in, and PIN
1. On Welcome, choose **Create family**, enter your name, email, password, and one to four children, then submit. Use **Sign in** for an existing account.
2. In parent mode, open **Security**. Enter a four-to-eight-digit **New PIN** and choose **Save PIN**.
3. Use **Kid mode** to hand over this device and **Grown-ups** to return. A configured PIN gates parent switches; your password signs you in.
Tip: If you forget your PIN, the unlock screen offers a password reset for the PIN. Keep credentials out of Buddy chat and help questions.

## Kid codes and devices
1. Open **Kids**, then **Children** on a phone, or **Children** in the desktop sidebar. Choose **Get a kid code** for the right child.
2. On the child's device, open Welcome, choose **I have a kid code**, enter the six-character **Kid code**, and submit.
3. Check for **Linked ✓**. Codes work once and expire after 15 minutes. Under **Devices**, choose **Unlink** to disconnect a device.
Tip: A six-character kid code signs in a device. An eight-character friend code only adds friends. Generate a new kid code after a child signs out.

## Send money
1. Open **Allowance** (under the Money tab on phones). Set **Weekly allowance ($)**, **Saved automatically (%)**, **Purchase limit without approval ($)**, and **Rewards go to**, then choose **Save rules**.
2. Under **Send money**, enter **Amount ($)** and an optional **Note**, then choose **Send money**. Record up to $500 at once; it goes straight into the child's Savings.
3. Check the next payout in **Allowance & limits**. The Guardian records scheduled allowance directly into Savings and Spending. **Overview** shows **Received this month**, including money you sent, money your child recorded, and automatic Apple Cash shortfall credits.
Tip: MoneyQuest records money; it never transfers real money. Send the actual money separately. Coordinate with your child so only one of you records each payment. Retrying a failed submission keeps the same receipt ID.

## Apple Cash and the Shortcut
1. Open **Children** and find **Apple Cash tracking** for the right child. Set a parent PIN first, then unlock parent mode.
2. Choose **Generate Shortcut token** and **Copy token**. Follow the setup guide at /help/apple-cash (also linked by **How to connect the iPhone Shortcut**).
3. Run the Shortcut connection test and check **Connected**. Rotating a token disconnects the previous Shortcut. **Unlink** disconnects it too.
4. Check **Apple Cash activity**. Purchases come out of Savings. If Savings is short, the missing amount is added automatically and you get a notification; no approval is needed.
Tip: The Shortcut records purchases already made in Wallet. It never sends Apple Cash. Do not record the same purchase manually as well. Never paste a Shortcut token into help or chat.

## Approvals
1. Open **Inbox** on a phone, or **Approvals** in the desktop sidebar, for the selected child.
2. Review each request and choose **Approve**, **Confirm**, or **Decline** as shown. Some notices use **Mark handled**.
3. Check **Recently decided** for results. If a purchase is waiting for child funds, help your child save more or decline it for now.
Tip: Approving a reached savings-goal purchase records the target from savings; it does not order anything or send real money. Both families and the receiving child must consent to a friendship.

## Goals & tasks
1. Open **Goals & tasks**. Use **Reward goal** and **Create goal** for a tracked reward, or **Assign a task** and **Assign task** for a task you will confirm.
2. Under **Savings goals**, enter the title, target, and picture, then choose **Create savings goal**. Use **Make current** to choose a live goal.
3. **Archive** puts a goal away. **Bring back** restores it. Choose another current goal before **Delete**. Bought goals remain memories.
Tip: Savings goals share the child's savings balance. Task points and money rewards are separate; rewards require approval and are then credited directly.

## Friends & privacy
1. Open **Friends & privacy** (under Kids on phones). Choose **Family time zone** for daily and weekly game rules.
2. Set **Ask me before postcard trades** and the Money Buddy switch for this child.
3. Review friend requests in **Approvals**. Use **Unfriend** to remove an approved friendship. In **Children**, use **Replace friend code** if needed.
Tip: Friends see first names, bears, postcards, and prewritten greetings. They do not see balances, allowance, transactions, or open chat. An incoming name and family display label appear only after the sender's parent consents.

## Children, profiles, and Account
1. Use the **Child** selector at the top of parent mode to choose whose account you are viewing.
2. Open **Children** to **Add a child** or edit a child's profile. Each child keeps their own allowance, bear, and adventures.
3. Open **Account** (under More on phones) to edit your name, **Family display name**, sign-in email, or password.
Tip: Changing the family display name changes the label shared with consented friends. Changing your password signs out other parent sessions, while kid devices stay paired.

## Guardian log
1. Open **Guardian log** (under More on phones) to read **Ledger Guardian** and its **Decision log**.
2. Turn on **Show all activity** to include other activity. Open **Transactions** for ledger rows and reversal status.
Tip: The Guardian follows schedules and approvals, prevents overdrafts, reverses duplicate postings, and asks about unusual activity. Real money stays with you.

## Buddy chats and AI
1. Open **Buddy chats** (under More on phones) to read **Money Buddy chats** for the selected child. Use **Load older** when offered.
2. Buddy can explain balances and goals, read wardrobe and travel progress, look up app instructions, write a savings plan, and propose a mission for your approval.
3. Open **Ask about MoneyQuest** for app help. Type a question and choose **Ask**. Answers use this guide; question and answer pairs are not saved in MoneyQuest.
Tip: AI can make mistakes. Buddy cannot move money or approve requests. With no AI key, guide lookup and a basic offline planner work. Turn child chat off in Friends & privacy when needed.

## Signing out
1. Use **Sign out** for this device, from the parent header or **Security**.
2. In **Security**, choose **Sign out everywhere**, then **Confirm sign out everywhere**, to revoke all family sessions, including kid devices and this device.
Tip: Children will need new kid codes after a family-wide sign-out. Switching to Kid mode keeps this parent session on the device, protected by your configured PIN.

## Data and privacy
1. Review child conversations in **Buddy chats** and activity in **Guardian log** and **Transactions**.
2. Keep emails, addresses, passwords, kid codes, and Shortcut tokens out of help and chat. Obvious personal details are redacted before AI calls; this is not a guarantee for every kind of private text.
3. Use **Devices** and **Unlink**, or **Sign out everywhere**, to revoke access you no longer want.
Tip: MoneyQuest stores account and game records on its server. Child chats are retained for parent review; parent help pairs are not stored. AI-enabled help sends a redacted question, guide excerpts, children's first names, the selected child, PIN-set status, and Shortcut connection status to the AI provider. It cannot delete data or change account settings for you.
