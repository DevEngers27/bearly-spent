# MoneyQuest for kids

## Home and your bear
1. Open **Home** to see <bear>, your money, and your goal.
2. Earn adventure points with missions and lessons. Points pay for trips and clothes; they are not money.
Tip: Saving helps <bear> grow. Your savings milestones stay yours.

## Talking to <bear>
1. Tap <bear> at home to talk. Type your question and tap **Send**.
2. Try **How do I…?** for help using MoneyQuest. Open **Chat** to read **Our chat**.
Tip: Your grown-up can read your chats. Keep passwords, addresses, and phone numbers private. Chat rests while <bear> is away, chatting is switched off, or your device is offline.

## Saving and spending
1. Tap **Save money** or **Spent money** on **Home** to open **My money**.
2. In **Save**, choose an amount and tap **Save it**. This moves spending money into savings.
3. In **Spent**, choose **Apple Cash** or **Spending money** under **Paid with**, enter the amount and what you bought, then tap **Record it**.
4. When your grown-up sends you money outside the app, open **Received**, enter the amount and sender, then tap **Add received money**. This adds to savings. Do not add allowance or rewards already recorded in the app.
5. Use **Cash diary** and **Add to diary** for cash outside the app. It does not change your app balances.
Tip: Apple Cash purchases deduct from savings automatically when your configured Shortcut reports them. Only record a purchase yourself if your Shortcut has not already recorded it. If savings are short, the purchase waits; recording received money retries waiting purchases. MoneyQuest records money; it does not send real money.

## Goals
1. On **Home**, tap **Switch goal**, or **My goals** if you have no current goal.
2. Fill in **Saving for** and **Target ($)**, pick a picture, and tap **Create goal**. Use **Save for this** to choose an existing goal.
3. When your current goal is reached, tap **Ask to buy it** on **Home**. Your grown-up decides.
Tip: All your goals share one savings pot. Switching goals does not move money.

## Missions and lessons
1. Open **Missions** and choose something under **Today**. Follow its hint to earn points.
2. Pick a lesson under **Next lessons** and answer its questions. Passed lessons move to **Finished · review, no points**.
3. Try **Weekly budget challenge** under **This week**. Plan your money across **Save**, **Needs**, **Fun**, and **Give**.
Tip: Budgeting is practice; it does not move money. Review lessons any time, but repeats earn no points.

## Trips and the Album
1. Earn enough points, then use the trip button on **Home** to send <bear> on a surprise trip. Only one trip can happen at a time.
2. When <bear> comes home, tap **Add to album**.
3. Open **Album**, then **My postcards**, to see your cards. Tap a postcard to turn it over. Open **Map** and tap a pin to explore a place.
Tip: Trips use points, never savings. Secret places turn up on trips as you build good saving habits. Swapped cards count as cards, but do not add places visited.

## Friends and swaps
1. Open **Album**, then **Friends**. Under **Add a friend**, enter your friend's eight-character **Friend code** and tap **Send**.
2. Both children and both grown-ups must say yes first. Use **Accept friend** for a request you want to accept.
3. With an approved friend, choose cards under **You give** and **You get**, then tap **Propose swap**. Your friend uses **Accept swap**; grown-ups check when required.
Tip: Find your own friend code by tapping your name at the top. It is different from a kid sign-in code. Friends see bears, postcards, and prewritten notes, never money. Secret cards cannot be swapped.

## Dress up
1. Tap **Dress up** on **Home** or on your profile.
2. Choose a wardrobe tab and an item. If it costs points, check the price and tap **Buy**, or **Not now** to wait.
3. Tap an item you own to wear it. **None** takes off an item; choose a scene to change home.
Tip: Some special items unlock at savings milestones and stay yours forever.

## Me page
1. Tap your name at the top, or **Me** in **Chat**, to open your profile.
2. Tap **Rename my bear**, type a name for <bear>, and tap **Save name**.
3. On a paired kid device, use **Sign out**, then **Confirm sign out**. **Stay here** cancels.
Tip: After signing out, ask your grown-up for a new kid code. Your grown-up can change your name and age.

## When something doesn't work
1. If you see **Offline**, check your connection with a grown-up. Your saved postcards are still there.
2. If a Buddy reply has a retry button, try it when you are connected and <bear> is home.
3. Ask a grown-up if a purchase, friend request, or reward is waiting.
Tip: If this guide does not answer your question, ask a grown-up. Never share a password or code in chat.
